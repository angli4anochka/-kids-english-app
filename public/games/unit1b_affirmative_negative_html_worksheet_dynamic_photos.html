Affirmative and negative forms
Theory

We use the verb to be (am / is / are) to talk about people and things.

Affirmative (positive)
I
am
he / she / it
is
we / you / they
are
Examples:   I am happy. They are students.
Negative
I
am not
he / she / it
isn’t
we / you / they
aren’t
Examples:   I am not tired. They aren’t teachers.
