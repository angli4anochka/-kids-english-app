9. Dan & Jo

Read / listen. Find 3 pieces of advice.
7 min
Listen for: Why is Jo’s dad installing an alarm?
Find: 3 pieces of advice.
Dan
Hi, Jo. What’s your dad doing?
Jo
He’s installing an alarm system.
Dan
What for?
Jo
There are burglars around here. They’re stealing things from people’s houses.
Dan
Really?
Jo
Yes, you should tell your parents.
Dan
They should probably install an alarm, too.
Jo
You should be careful with your keys. Don’t lose them or leave them near an open window.
Dan
I didn’t think of that.
Jo
There’s a lot of crime in cities these days.
Dan
We should be careful.
Jo
Better safe than sorry!
What is Jo’s dad doing?
What problems do burglars cause?
What advice does Jo give Dan?
Do you agree with Jo? Why?

10. SHOULD / SHOULDN’T

Rule + all advice practice from the handouts.
7 min
SHOULD + base verb = advice


✓ You should lock the door.
✗ You should to lock the door.
✗ You should locks the door.

SHOULDN’T + base verb: You shouldn’t open the door.
should = совет · must = необходимость / обязанность
