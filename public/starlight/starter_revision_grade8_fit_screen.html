05 · ASSOCIATIONS

Build the sport in your head

The idea is association, not one “correct” category. A sport can belong to several ideas at once.

Examples

windsurfing → water + air / wind + speed + balance
paragliding → air + height + courage
street luge → land / road + speed + danger

Model: I associate windsurfing with water, wind and speed. You also need good balance.

Now make your own
