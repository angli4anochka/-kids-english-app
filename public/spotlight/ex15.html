the verb ‘have got’

We often use ‘have got’, especially in spoken English, and especially in
the present.

This does not change the meaning: we use have/has got like have/has
to talk about possession etc.

  • •I have got = I have
  • •Have you got? = Do you have?
  • •She hasn’t got = She doesn’t have
I’ve got a cat.
She’s got a sister.
I haven’t got a car.
You’ve got beautiful eyes.
Has she got a dog?
Have you got a cold?
